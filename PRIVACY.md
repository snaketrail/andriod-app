# Privacy Policy for AI Brief

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app **AI Brief** (package name `com.snaketrail.aibrief`), published by **snaketrail** ("the developer").

## Summary

AI Brief does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

## What the app does

AI Brief shows a list of recent news stories about artificial intelligence, each with a short AI-written summary and a link to the original article.

## Data the app collects

**None.** The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any other personal information. It does not require you to sign in.

## Data stored on your device

The following is saved only on your own device, so the app can work offline and remember your choices:

- the most recently downloaded list of stories;
- the stories you bookmark with "Save";
- which news sources you have switched on or off.

This information never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by using "Clear cached stories" in the app's settings, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Network requests

- **Downloading the news feed.** The app downloads one file containing the stories and summaries. The file is hosted on GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and basic technical request information. The developer has no access to this information. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Opening articles.** When you tap "Read full article" or a source's name, the page opens in your browser. Those websites are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies, which the developer does not control.

## AI-generated summaries

The summaries are created in advance by the developer using an AI service, based only on publicly available news articles. Nothing about you or your use of the app is sent to any AI service. Summaries are generated automatically and may contain mistakes; the linked original article is the authoritative source.

## Sharing with third parties

The app does not share any user data with third parties, because it does not collect any.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
